    Anant Gupta

    I build small, fast tools and take apart the big ones to see how they work.

    I'm a first-year Chemical Engineering student at BITS Pilani, Hyderabad, from Noida. Most of my free time goes into C, shell scripts and Linux: compiling my own window manager, terminal and launcher, then automating the whole setup so it rebuilds from one command.

    This site works like that setup. Press 1 to 5 to switch workspace, j/k to change focus and Enter to swap a window into the main area. Press f to make the windows float, then drag them by their title bars and resize them from the bottom-right corner. In tiled mode, drag the dividers between windows, or press h/l for left/right and i/m for up/down. Press / to search, p to launch a project, and ? at any time to see every keybinding.
